About Us

Complete Insurance Solution was established in 2016 by Mr. Sameer Mogre with a mission to provide reliable, comprehensive, and customer-focused insurance services. Since its inception, the firm has been dedicated to helping individuals, families, and businesses secure their financial future through customized insurance solutions.

Mr. Sameer Mogre brings over 15 years of extensive experience in the insurance industry. His professional journey includes working in reputed Third-Party Administrator (TPA) organizations and insurance brokerage firms, where he gained valuable expertise in insurance operations, claims management, customer service, and risk assessment. He also served as a Branch Manager for more than six years, developing strong leadership and client relationship management skills.

Born and brought up in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh, Mr. Mogre holds an MBA in Finance from Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), Indore, along with M.Com. (Accounts & Taxation) and M.Com. (Management) from Vikram University, Ujjain. His educational background, combined with practical industry experience, enables him to provide informed and effective insurance guidance to clients.

Complete Insurance Solution offers a wide range of insurance products from leading insurance companies, including life, health, motor, property, travel, and business insurance. The firm is associated with major insurers such as LIC, The New India Assurance, National Insurance, Bajaj GIC, Generali Central, Star Health, and other reputed insurance providers.

At Complete Insurance Solution, we believe that insurance is more than just a policy—it is a promise of protection and peace of mind. Our commitment is to deliver personalized service, expert advice, and prompt support, ensuring that every client receives the right coverage for their unique needs.
